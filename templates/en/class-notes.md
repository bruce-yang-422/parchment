## 1. Key points

> [!abstract] Three-sentence summary
> 1. e.g. An object with no net force stays at rest or moves in a straight line at constant speed (inertia).
> 2. e.g. Net force equals mass times acceleration.
> 3. e.g. Every action has an equal and opposite reaction.

## 2. Key terms

Inertia
: The tendency of an object to keep its state of motion; more mass means more inertia.

Net force
: The vector sum of all forces acting on an object.

## 3. Formulas

Newton’s second law: $F = ma$

$$
a = \frac{F_{\text{net}}}{m}
$$

| Symbol | Meaning | Unit |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| $F$ | Force | N (newton) |
| $m$ | Mass | kg |
| $a$ | Acceleration | m/s² |

## 4. Worked example

> [!example] Example 1
> A 2 kg object is pushed with a force of 10 N. What is its acceleration?
>
> $a = \frac{10}{2} = 5 \text{ m/s}^2$
