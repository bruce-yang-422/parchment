Read .md files on your phone

Parchment is a Markdown and plain-text reader. When someone sends you a .md file, open it in Parchment and you get a formatted document instead of a wall of symbols.

We are looking for Android users to join Google Play internal and closed testing. Please use the app for at least 14 days and tell us what works and what could be better.

Sign up See the features

Free, no account needed. Documents open only on your phone and are never uploaded.

Android only — no iOS version yet

Parchment is currently available only for Android. It cannot be installed on iPhone or iPad, so iPhone users cannot join this test. You can use the web app to read Markdown in your browser instead.

Features

Clear Markdown reading

Tables, code, math and flowcharts are fully formatted. Front matter such as title, author and tags is shown as a tidy table.

Open any local file

Open a single file or a whole notes folder and move between notes. Files you receive in WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail or a file manager open straight in Parchment.

Jump to any section

Long documents get an automatic outline — tap a heading to jump straight there.

Document details at a glance

File name, size, modified time, word count, task progress and tags in one place, plus one-tap copy of the name, location or full text.

And more
  • Five color themes and several fonts; text size and line spacing are adjustable and remembered.
  • Tick tasks directly and save them back to the original file after you confirm.
  • Interface in 8 languages: English, Traditional and Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German and Spanish.
  • Works offline; it only goes online to show web images or download fonts.

How to join internal and closed testing

  1. Fill in the formEnter a nickname and the Google account email you use for Google Play.
  2. Wait for the invitationWe add your email to the internal or closed testing list and send you an invitation.
  3. Accept with your linkSigning up does not add you to the test yet — open the link in the invitation to join the Google Play test.
  4. Install from Google PlayAfter joining, install Parchment from Google Play; updates arrive automatically.
  5. Use it for 14 days and share feedbackUse it whenever you need to read a .md file, and tell us about any problems or ideas.

The sign-up form is in Chinese. Fill in three things: 稱呼或暱稱 (nickname), Google Play 使用的 Google 帳號 Email (the Google account email you use for Google Play), and 是否有 Android 手機 — choose 有 (yes). Then tick 我已閱讀並同意 (I have read and agree) and submit.

Requirements
  • An Android phone with Google Play (Android 7.0 or later). There is no iOS version, so iPhone and iPad cannot take part.
  • A Google account — the one you use to sign in to Google Play.

Your data

Sign-up details (nickname and Google account email) are used only for test invitations, eligibility checks and test communication. They are never made public or used for advertising, and data no longer needed is deleted within 90 days after the test ends. The full notice is in the sign-up form.

The Parchment app itself needs no account and collects no usage data. See the privacy policy.

No Google Play?

You can also download the APK from GitHub, open it on your phone and allow the installation. The downloaded version and the Google Play version cannot update each other; uninstall first if you later switch to Google Play.
